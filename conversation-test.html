Current boundary

The free AI bridge is optional and activates only after its Cloudflare Worker and public Turnstile sitekey are configured. Until then, local parsing remains available. AI interprets messages but never reads saved ledger rows or saves transactions.

AI DATA BOUNDARY

When enabled, Smart Ledger sends your current and recent messages to Groq for interpretation. It does not send saved ledger rows, balances or report totals. Groq says inference is not retained by default, but may temporarily log inputs and outputs for reliability or abuse investigations (up to 30 days).
